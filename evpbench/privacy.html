EVP Bench privacy policy

EVP Bench is made by Neeberman Digital. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information we collect

EVP Bench does not collect, store on a server, share, or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. Nothing you record is sent to Neeberman Digital.

Information stored on your device

Recordings, marks, and the library are saved only on this phone. They are not uploaded. Android backup is turned off for the app. Uninstalling EVP Bench removes them. If you use the share sheet, the wav is handed to the app you pick, and what happens next is covered by that app.

Microphone

The microphone is used only while you are recording or the tripwire is listening. The audio stays on this phone. A notification stays visible while the microphone is open.

Internet

The bench screen talks only to this phone, at 127.0.0.1. That connection does not leave the device and does not send a recording or any personal information anywhere.

What the bench is not

EVP Bench keeps sound as sound. It does not turn noise into words, and it does not detect ghosts, spirits, or messages. A mark is a place in the audio. Do not use a mark to make a decision, or as contact with someone you have lost.

Children

EVP Bench is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Price

EVP Bench is a paid app. It has no ads and no in-app purchases.
